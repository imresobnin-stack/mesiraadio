⌨️ Keyboard shortcuts
SpacePlay / pause. The biggest key on your keyboard finally earns its keep. /Jump to search — no trek to the mouse required. rRandom station. Let the dice decide. vCards ⇄ list. Sometimes you want the big picture, sometimes just names. mWorld map. Pick a dot and the cactus hops over — with the local time, so you know who's up at 4 am. sScan like an old radio: random stations for a few seconds each. Space = “stay here”. kClock screen: big clock, station and song on pure black. Inside it f = fullscreen, Space = pause, Esc or k = back. Perfect for a desert night. h“What was that song?” — every track you've heard, one click from YouTube or Spotify. EscCloses windows (this one too).
🥚 Easter eggs

Hit “peek” when curiosity wins.

🌵The cactus is ticklish.peek

Click it — it spins, waves or takes a bow. Just don't overdo it: five quick clicks in a row and it gets prickly.

🪩Sometimes there's a secret party in the desert.peek

Enter the Konami code ↑ ↑ ↓ ↓ ← → ← → B A or click the logo seven times fast. 20 seconds of disco and confetti.

🍯The search box has a sweet tooth.peek

Type “honey” and watch what drips from the sky. “Ants” brings a whole marching column.

🎲Even dice have their limits.peek

Ten rolls within a minute and the die overheats — it smokes and the cactus gets dizzy.

😴Silence makes it sleepy.peek

When nothing is playing, the cactus yawns. After ten minutes it falls into a deep sleep — and wakes up startled when the music starts.

🔇Don't mute it.peek

Turn the volume to zero and the cactus gets grumpy. Turn it back up and all is forgiven.

👀Someone is watching you.peek

Move your mouse — the cactus follows the cursor with its eyes. And now and then it blinks.

💃Every genre has its own moves.peek

Rock, electronic, jazz… the cactus dances to whatever is playing.

🕰️The cactus has a daily rhythm.peek

Fresh in the morning, focused on weekdays, chilled on weekends, mellow in the evening. From 11 pm to 6 am it puts on a nightcap.

🎄Winter gets chilly, even for a cactus.peek

In December (or when a Christmas station plays) it snows and the cactus gets a scarf.

🎃The end of October gets spooky.peek

From 27 to 31 October the cactus wears a witch hat, a jack-o'-lantern glows on the speaker and bats circle overhead. Type “boo” into the search — if you dare.

⏰One letter in the name isn't a letter.peek

The O in WORLD RADIO is actually a clock — and it tells the real time. Click it: the screen goes black, big numbers glow and the seconds tick around a ring of dots, with the station and current song below. Double-click for fullscreen, the grey cross in the corner takes you back.
